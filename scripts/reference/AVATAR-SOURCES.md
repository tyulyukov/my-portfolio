# Avatar source assets

The current avatar replaces the former MakeHuman head mesh. Its checked-in likeness data is `likeness/face.json`: 468 XYZ vertices fitted to the original portrait landmarks and profile depth and 898 triangles from the MediaPipe canonical face topology. The generated front, profile, and back textures are also checked in under `likeness/`; the personal source photographs are not published.

The canonical topology comes from the [MediaPipe canonical face model](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/a908d668c730da128dfa8d9f6bd25d519d006692/mediapipe/modules/face_geometry/data/canonical_face_model.obj), licensed under Apache License 2.0. A copy of that license is included at [likeness/LICENSE.txt](likeness/LICENSE.txt). It covers the MediaPipe topology; the generated portrait textures have the separate provenance described below.

Landmarks from the generated front texture provide facial UV placement and initial relative depth. Side and rear textures use projections aligned to the reference portraits. A profile trace refines the midline depth. This is a fitted likeness reference, not a scan or a guarantee of exact likeness.

`front.png`, `profile.png`, and `back.png` were generated by ImageGen from user-supplied front, profile, and back personal photographs. The photographs themselves are not included.

The rendered head, hair, neck, torso, glasses, hands, and MacBook are baked and exported by Blender 5.2.1. Rebuilding requires Blender and the checked-in data only; no MakeHuman download or private reference photographs are required.
